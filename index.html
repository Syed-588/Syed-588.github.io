Projects

Does greenwashing affect revenue growth?

Data Science DeCal, UC Berkeley, Fall 2026

I cleaned company data from the energy, industrials, and utilities sectors (2011 to 2024) and built a model to test whether a company’s environmental claims affect how fast its revenue grows compared to similar companies.

What I found: greenwashing did not reliably predict revenue growth, while company size had a clearer effect.

Python, Pandas, NumPy, statsmodels, Matplotlib, Seaborn

AI chatbot with Google Gemini

Personal project, Jul 2025

A Python chatbot that uses the Google Gemini API to answer questions and remember the conversation. It also uses Sentence-Transformers to measure how close in meaning two messages are.

Python, Google Gemini API, Sentence-Transformers
